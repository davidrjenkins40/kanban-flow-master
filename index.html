Skip to Game Board

Kanban Flow Master is a 6-column Kanban puzzle game. Pull ready cards from New to Approved, Analysis, Development, Testing, and Done. Use Left and Right Arrow keys or keys 1 through 6 to move between columns. Use Up and Down Arrow keys to choose a card. Press Enter or Space to advance a ready card to the next column, or single-click or drag ready cards with a pointer. Press P to pause, M to toggle sound, V to switch avatar, and H for full help.

Kanban Flow Master — How to Play & Accessibility

Guide work smoothly across a 6-column Kanban board (NEW → APPROVED → ANALYSIS → DEVELOPMENT → TESTING → DONE) across a 5-day workweek, culminating in Friday's 3rd-party supplier boss fight against Apex Vendor Corp.

Pointer Controls (Single-Tap & Drag)

  • Single-Click / Single-Tap (No Dragging Required): Tap any [READY ▸] card (or any card in NEW) to immediately advance it one column to the right.
  • Drag-and-Drop: You can also drag ready cards smoothly into the next column to the right.

Keyboard Controls (Focus the Game Board)

  • ← → or A D — Move focus between the 6 columns.
  • 1–6 — Jump focus directly to columns 1 through 6.
  • ↑ ↓ or W S — Move focus between cards in the current column.
  • Enter or Space — Advance the focused ready card one column right (or take over from Attract Mode).
  • P — Pause / Resume  |  M — Mute / Unmute Sound  |  V — Cycle Avatar  |  H or ? — Toggle Help  |  Esc — Close Help / Resume.

Card & Column Indicators (Color + Text + Icon)

  • [READY ▸] + Checkmark: Work is finished in this column—advance it to the right!
  • [BOIL!] + Thermometer + Dashed Border: Completed card sat too long and is draining points—move it immediately!
  • [BLOCKER] + Octagon + Hazard Stripes: Slows other cards in the same column by 50% until cleared.
  • [CYBER!] + Siren + Double Border: Urgent security incident with a countdown timer—expedite to DONE!
  • [SUPPLIER] + Broken Link: Day 5 Boss incident—deliver to DONE to deal 1 HP damage to Apex Vendor Corp!
  • [WIP FULL] / [FROZEN]: Column is over its WIP limit (slower work) or frozen by a Boss API outage.
